WHY JOIN A SAFARI GROUP?

Authentic Discipleship in Community

Safari Groups are the heart of pastoral care and discipleship at CITAM Buruburu. They provide a warm, intimate setting where believers study God's Word, pray for one another, build lasting friendships, and support each other through life's joys and challenges.

Whether you live in Buruburu Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, Phase 4, Phase 5, Kariobangi South, Umoja, or surrounding areas, there is a Safari Group near you.
