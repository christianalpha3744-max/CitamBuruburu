About CITAM Buruburu

Knowing God and Making Him Known through worship, discipleship, and community transformation.

OUR STORY & HERITAGE

A legacy of faith and service

CITAM Buruburu is an established CITAM assembly in Nairobi, proclaiming the gospel and serving the Buruburu community and the wider Nairobi East region.

On 25 May 2025, during its 18th anniversary, the church launched its new 4,000-seater Intercooler Sanctuary — the first assembly project launched under CITAM’s Together In Generosity (TIG) initiative.

Sanctuary groundbreaking — 14 June 2023
Official launch — 25 May 2025 (18th anniversary)
First assembly project under Together In Generosity
